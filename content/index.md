# Map of Mathematics

Welcome to the Mathematics Knowledge Base. This wiki scales from the fundamental axioms of logic to advanced modern structures. The roadmap below tracks the logical dependencies of mathematical disciplines.

## 🧭 Foundations & Logic
* [[Mathematical Logic]] — Propositional calculus, predicates, and proof techniques
* [[Set Theory]] — Axiomatic set theory (ZFC), cardinals, and ordinals
* [[Category Theory]] — The universal language of mathematical structures

##    Core Pillars

### 1. Algebra
* [[Abstract Algebra]] 
	* [[Group Theory]] — Symmetry, subgroups, and Lagrange's theorem
	* [[Ring Theory]] — Ideals, factor rings, and integral domains
	* [[Field Theory]] — Galois theory and field extensions
* [[Linear Algebra]] — Vector spaces, linear transformations, matrices, and [[Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors]]

### 2. Analysis
* [[Real Analysis]] — Limits, continuity, sequences, and Riemann integrals
* [[Complex Analysis]] — Holomorphic functions, contour integration, and residue theorem
* [[Functional Analysis]] — Hilbert spaces, Banach spaces, and operator theory
* [[Differential Equations]] — Ordinary (ODE) and Partial (PDE) dynamical systems

### 3. Geometry & Topology
* [[General Topology]] — Metric spaces, topological spaces, compactness, and connectedness
* [[Differential Geometry]] — Manifolds, curvature, tensors, and Riemannian metrics
* [[Algebraic Topology]] — Homotopy, homology groups, and fundamental groups

### 4. Discrete Mathematics & Number Theory
* [[Number Theory]] — Divisibility, prime numbers, modular arithmetic, and diophantine equations
* [[Combinatorics]] — Graph theory, counting principles, and generating functions

---

## 🚀 Applied Mathematics
* [[Probability and Statistics]] — Measure-theoretic probability, random variables, and inference
* [[Numerical Analysis]] — Approximation algorithms and error analysis
* [[Optimization Theory]] — Convex optimization, linear programming, and calculus of variations
